# 📚 Biblioteca Municipal "Jesús Lara" · Catálogo en línea

**Find any book in the library in seconds, from your phone or computer.**

The Biblioteca Municipal "Jesús Lara" in Cochabamba now has its catalogue online. Search almost 40,000 books by author or title, and see right away where to find them on the shelf.

👉 **[Open the catalogue](https://biblioteca-jesuslara-rbr.streamlit.app)**

## Why you'll like it

- **Fast.** Type a name or a title and the results appear instantly.
- **Forgiving.** Accents don't matter, words can go in any order, and small typos still find the book. "garcia marquez" finds *García Márquez*.
- **Everything you need to locate the book.** Each result shows the author, title, Dewey number and Cutter code, which is what the librarian uses to find it on the shelf.
- **Free and open to everyone.** No account or sign-up is needed.

## How to use it

1. Open the catalogue.
2. Choose **Autor** or **Título**.
3. Type part of the name or title.
4. Write down the Dewey and Cutter numbers and bring them to the library.
